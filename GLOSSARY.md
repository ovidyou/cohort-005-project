# Course Platform

A marketplace where instructors publish video courses and learners buy and watch them.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund**:
Money for a Purchase going back to the learner who paid it. An approved Refund ends the learner's access; the Purchase stays on record as refunded.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, in their own words, for a Refund on one of their Purchases. Someone reviews it by hand; it is always exactly one of Requested, Approved or Declined, and only an Approved one produces a Refund.
_Avoid_: Cancellation request, Reversal request, Pending Chargeback, open refund, pending refund

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen days from the day of a Purchase during which the learner may raise a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation window

**Eligible for a Refund**:
A Purchase is eligible when it is inside its Refund Window, the learner has watched less than two hours and completed less than half of the course's lessons, it is not a claimed team seat, and it has no Refund Request that is Requested.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, refund offer, cancellable

**Refundable Amount**:
What a Refund for a Purchase gives back: exactly the amount paid for that Purchase.
_Avoid_: List price, refund price

**Chargeback**:
A dispute a cardholder raises with their bank instead of with us; the bank, not the platform, decides it. Not modelled today, and never a synonym for Refund.
